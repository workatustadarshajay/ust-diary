1. AI Guided Conversation
Instead of generating everything at once, AI asks one question at a time:

What happened today?
How did it affect you?
What did you learn?
What do you want to do tomorrow?
At the end, it creates a complete diary entry for your approval.

2. Custom Template Manager
Allow users to:

Create their own templates
Rename templates
Edit template questions
Delete templates
Duplicate templates
Organize templates by category
Save an existing diary entry as a template
This is the natural next step after the current predefined template feature.

3. Writing Streaks
Add calendar and dashboard statistics:

Current writing streak
Longest streak
Total entries
Total words written
Days written this month
Monthly completion percentage
Keep this subtle so the diary does not feel like a productivity competition.

4. Mood Calendar
Show mood colors directly on calendar days:

Green: positive
Blue: calm
Yellow: focused
Orange: tired
Red: heavy or stressed
Also show a monthly mood summary.

5. Daily Reminders
Add optional reminders:

Write at a chosen time
Reminder when today's page is empty
Weekly reflection reminder
Monthly review reminder
For the first version, browser notifications are enough. Push notifications can come later.

6. Entry Attachments
Support:

Images
PDFs
Screenshots
Voice notes
Small files
For a diary, images and voice notes would be particularly valuable.

7. Secure Private Mode
Because diary content is sensitive, add:

App lock with PIN
Lock after inactivity
Hide diary previews
Private mode for selected entries
Encrypted local draft storage
Do not store a plain-text PIN. Use a secure hash or Web Crypto API.

AI Features

Generate an optional weekly report:

What happened this week
What went well
What was difficult

Unfinished tasks

“When did I last write about my exam?”
“What has been taking most of my time?”
“Which days did I feel productive?”
“What goals have I mentioned?”
“Show entries related to UST work.”
The AI should answer only from diary entries and include linked dates.
